# Christian AI Field Tracker

Living register of Christian institutions, research centers, ministry networks, startups, products, conferences, and journals working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and Christian faith.

- First seeded: 2026-09-10
- Canonical repo: https://github.com/ainranian/christian-ai-tracker
- Cadence: weekly, Sunday 07:00 Africa/Johannesburg
- Rule: no unsourced claims; every change needs a full URL
- History: append-only. Do not overwrite prior weekly snapshots.

## Status codes

- `active` — publicly operating
- `proposed` — announced, not yet operating
- `event` — conference/symposium
- `publication` — journal, handbook, statement, encyclical
- `prototype` — hackathon or early build
- `unverified-method` — product exists; training stack not published
- `dormant` — no public activity found in last 90 days
- `closed` — confirmed shutdown

## Categories

B Protestant / evangelical / ecumenical research
C Digital theology centers, journals, working groups
D Bible translation and missions engineering
E Faith-tech platforms, incubators, networks
F Consumer Bible / prayer / companion apps
